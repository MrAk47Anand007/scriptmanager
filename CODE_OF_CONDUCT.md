# Code of Conduct

ScriptManager contributors and maintainers should make discussions welcoming, respectful, and useful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent, ask for clarification, and accept reasonable feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep issue and pull request discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal insults, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks you to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/MrAk47Anand007). Include links or screenshots and a short description of what happened. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports as privately as practical, may request more information, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation. The response will depend on the behavior and its impact. This code applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and other project spaces.
